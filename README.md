# Digital Marketing Checklist for Kolkata Businesses

A free, practical checklist to audit your own digital marketing before you spend more money on it. Written for small and mid-sized local businesses in Kolkata, including clinics, coaching centres, real estate, retail, and service companies.

Use it as a self-audit, or to compare what different agencies promise against what actually matters.

> **Maintained by [99Graphics Design](https://99graphicsdesign.com/digital-marketing-agency-in-kolkata/)**, a Kolkata-based digital marketing agency founded in 2009. Suggestions and corrections are welcome through issues and pull requests.

---

## How to use this checklist

1. Copy the checklist into a doc or spreadsheet.
2. Tick off what you already have.
3. Fix the unchecked items from top to bottom. The order matters.
4. Review again every quarter.

---

## 1. Website basics

- [ ] Site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Phone number and enquiry form visible without scrolling
- [ ] Each service has its own page with clear details
- [ ] Pages explain location, pricing approach, and process
- [ ] Mobile layout tested on a real phone
- [ ] Contact form actually sends and you receive the enquiry
- [ ] Clear call-to-action on every important page

## 2. Tracking and measurement

- [ ] Google Analytics 4 installed
- [ ] Google Search Console verified
- [ ] Calls, form submissions, and WhatsApp clicks tracked as conversions
- [ ] You know which channel brought your last 10 enquiries
- [ ] You have admin access to every account (not just the agency)

## 3. Google Business Profile (Local SEO)

- [ ] Profile claimed and verified
- [ ] Correct primary and secondary categories
- [ ] Accurate name, address, phone, hours, and website link
- [ ] Services listed clearly
- [ ] Recent, real photos added
- [ ] Reviews requested from customers regularly
- [ ] Reviews replied to
- [ ] Business details match your website exactly

> Google explains that local results are mainly influenced by **relevance**, **distance**, and **prominence**.

## 4. SEO

- [ ] Target searches are tied to real enquiries, not just traffic
- [ ] Each target search has one matching page
- [ ] Page titles and descriptions are written for people
- [ ] Internal links connect related pages
- [ ] No broken pages or crawl errors in Search Console
- [ ] Helpful content answers questions customers actually ask
- [ ] Competitor pages reviewed for gaps

## 5. Google Ads

- [ ] Conversion tracking is working before spending begins
- [ ] Search terms report reviewed regularly
- [ ] Negative keywords added to block irrelevant clicks
- [ ] Locations and schedules match your real service area
- [ ] Each ad group sends traffic to a matching landing page
- [ ] Cost per lead tracked, not just cost per click
- [ ] Budget reviewed against lead quality

## 6. Reporting

- [ ] Reports show enquiries, calls, form fills, conversion rate, and cost per lead
- [ ] Reports list what was done this month
- [ ] Reports explain what changes next month and why
- [ ] Percentage claims include a time period and data source

## Questions to ask any agency

| Topic | Question |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Who will manage my account day to day? |
| Access | Will I keep access to Google Ads, Analytics, Search Console, and Business Profile? |
| Measurement | How will enquiries be tracked? |
| Reporting | What exactly will I see each month? |
| Strategy | Do you review my business before recommending a package? |
| Pricing | Is ad spend included in your fee? |
| Honesty | Do you guarantee rankings or leads? (If yes, be careful.) |
